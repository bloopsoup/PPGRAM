# PPGRAM

> Reminding you that every **p**retty **p**icture is best enjoyed with others!

<br>

Each day, one person is chosen at random to share a snapshot of their life with others.
Pictures could be of food, dry wall, darkness, or any other staple of life.

Who will come up next?
Who will be the next victim of social interaction with their friends?
It is almost impossible to know.
